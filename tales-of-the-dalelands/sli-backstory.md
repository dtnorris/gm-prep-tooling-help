Sli - Backstory

Mother was barely legal sex worker, gave Sli to local temple Tempus to be raised with other kids away from the troubled life. After some time, the temple was ransacked by unknown gang & the order was all killed. The kids were saved by one of the older kids, Fiona, who woke them all & hid them in the woods.

After that, they continued living in the damaged temple, with Fiona taking on a mother/leader role. They stole, grifted, conned etc. to get by, and many of them found out their skills were good. They started being known as Fis Phoenix. They were family & gang, and eventually earned some renown.

The Cult of the Dragon recruited them for a few jobs & got along well. We all became initiates and started getting more & more serious (and dangerous) jobs.

Meanwhile, Sli has gotten back in contact with her mom, Twinkle Star (Star for short), who has risen in her own underworld domain by taking control of a string of brothels. Star is sad Sli ended up in “the life” despite her sacrifice. Makes Sli start to consider her life choices & talks to Fiona about it. Fiona says the jobs will come and go, but they’re family no matter what Sli does.

The next day, while still considering all this, Sli goes to the market to pick (pocket) her lunch. Ethel clocks her & they get to talking, and they quickly form a mentor/friendship bond.

Sli doesn’t officially leave the Cult of the Dragon, but she stops taking the more serious jobs, and stays pretty low level while her phoenix family advances in their ranks.

Previously, Sli had joined the Wardens to do recon on a previous job, but now is starting to consider helping them more for real.
