## Zeilgor

Zeilgor is opening magical portals across the Dalelands to spread the domain & influence of the Ostoyan Empire into a new World!

## Vadoma

Vadoma is collecting powerful secrets as well as powerful spell components in an effort to prepare a ritual that she can use to replace Lord Ulath and take over the rule of Deeping Dale and Highmoon.

## Moander / Alias

Moander, god of corruption, has begun stirring again. After a defeat and a slumber various distractions and outside threats to The Dalelands have made space. Alias has been away and sources of corruption in the forests have been allowed to fester.
