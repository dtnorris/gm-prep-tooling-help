  
Mists rise across the Dalelands and rumors abound of the many treasures to be discovered. Nestled deep within the Heartlands of the Forgotten Realms, The Dalelands is a land of classic fantasy tradition. Every village has an inn or tavern where a retired adventurer tells stories about the treasure that got away. An ancient primeval forest dominates the landscape filled with monsters and long-forgotten magic. What secrets might you discover? What scheming plots might you foil?

## Ancient Truths of The Dalelands

* The Cormanthor is an immense primeval forest holding many secrets.   
* The people of The Dalelands are independent and hardy made up of all kinds of folk.  
* Ancient and powerful secrets lie in Cormanthor.  
* The young realm of Sembia seeks to expand its rule.  
* Many dragons inhabit the Thunder peaks hoarding treasure and troubling nearby Dales.

## The Heroes We Have

Independent for over a thousand years, residents of The Dalelands are proud of their independence and highly self-sufficient. The characters are all Wardens of Deepingdale. You might be a long-time resident of Deepingdale, a recent arrival seeking to earn your place, or a previously retired Warden who has recently returned. Whatever your personal reason all Wardens share a common bond of trust and  fellowship. Unless called upon by Lord Ulath due to some unusually great need, Wardens are free folk expected to care for themselves and others. It is common for Wardens from time to time to join other groups and support other causes.

## You Begin in Highmoon

The capital of Deepingdale, Highmoon, is a walled town on the East Way. Thousands of elves and humans call the town home, demonstrating Deepingdale’s commitment to harmony. Lord Ulath, the ruler of Deepingdale for almost two centuries, makes his home in the Tower of the Rising Moon within Highmoon. Lord Ulath is famed for his great feasts, music, and other cultural traditions, rather than as a militant leader. An informal militia called the Wardens of Deepingdale protects the dale when necessary.  
	Notable locations include the **Leaves of Learning**, the **Tower of the Rising Moon**, and the **Teapot Temple & Tavern**.

## Your Character in the World

Characters begin at **2nd level**. You can choose any character options from the 5.5(2024) *Player’s Handbook*. We will be playing with (all) the *Nimble 5e* rules modifications contained in the *Nimble 5e* pdf packet. I am *also* more than happy to play with **any** other published 5e material. Other WotC expansion books, 3rd party (Kobold Press, Ghostfire Gaming/etc), or even Homebrew content\! For these sources I just wish to just discuss, review and consider them case by case. But 99% likely I will approve. I just want a discussion to make sure it fits in our world/games and enjoy understanding it with you\!  
	**Your character has joined with your companions to help a tavern owner clear a flooded basement. strangely the water is... Salty? And the tavern is far from any coast\!**  
	Build well-rounded characters able to interact with the denizens of The Dalelands, explore ancient ruins, and battle strange beasts lurking in the wilds.

## Your Group Patron

Your characters have come together on the request from a notable patron of The Dalelands. As a group, we will select from one of the following group patrons.  
	***Lord Theremen Ulath.*** Elven Ruler of Deepingdale, Theremen works with Wardens to investigate troubling rumors and generally keep his people safe.   
	***Hlin Trollbane.*** Retired shield dwarf bounty hunter, came to Highmoon some time ago escaping a dark past and now has taken it upon herself to bring justice where it has failed in the night.  
	***Thanali Graysteel.*** A human priestess who oversees the Leaves of Learning temple to Oghma. Seeks to steward the library, and help others with it, one of the most famous libraries in all of Faerun.

## Safety Tools

This game will be generally PG-13. Adventures will feature heroic fantasy, exploration, and dungeons\!  
	***Lines***. This game will not contain physical violence towards children, unwanted sexual contact, animal abuse or cruelty, PC-initiated torture, inter-PC violence, or inter-PC betrayal.  
	***Veils.*** Cannibalism, mental assault, ritual sacrifice, kidnapping, consensual sex, enemy-initiated torture, or parasitic invasion will be “veiled” off-screen.  
	If at any time you don’t feel comfortable with the content or direction of the game, say **“pause for a second”** and we will stop the game and address your concerns.
